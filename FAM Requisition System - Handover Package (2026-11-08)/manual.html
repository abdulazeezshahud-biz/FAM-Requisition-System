01 Getting started

The Requisition System is where FAM prepares purchases and expenses, collects the required signatures, records goods received and payment, and keeps every supporting document for audit — all in one place, on any device.

Signing in

  1. Open the system in your web browser. Your administrator will give you the web address (you can bookmark it or add it to your phone's home screen).
  2. Enter your username and password, then choose Sign in. These are personal to you — every action you take is recorded under your name.
  3. You land on the Dashboard. What you can see and do depends on your role (next section).
Good to know

Everything you do syncs instantly across devices. Prepare a requisition on your office computer, and an approver sees it on their phone a moment later.

Forgot your password, or locked out?

Contact an administrator — they can set a new password for you from Admin → Users in seconds. After several wrong passwords in a row, sign-in pauses for 10 minutes to protect your account; wait, then try again.

02 Roles & access

Your role decides which parts of the system you use. If a button described in this manual isn't visible to you, your role doesn't include that action.

RoleWhat you can do
ViewerRead-only. See requisitions, their progress, and reports. Cannot create or approve.
ApproverEverything a viewer can do, plus sign the approval steps assigned to you (e.g. Department Head, General Secretary, President), and raise your own expense requests.
FinanceCreate requisitions, send approval links, record paper signatures, goods received, payment and Odoo posting, upload documents, combine expenses, and run reports.
AdminFull access, plus the Admin tab — system settings (numbering, tax rates, approval chain, low-value rule, lists, Odoo) and managing users.

An approver is also linked to one or more signing rights — the specific chain steps they're allowed to sign (for example, the General Secretary can only sign the "General Secretary" step). This is set up per person by an administrator.

03 The dashboard

The dashboard is your at-a-glance overview. It's the fastest way to see what needs your attention and where money is going.

Headline figures

Total requisitions, total value (shown per currency), how many are awaiting approval, and how many are waiting on you specifically.

Pipeline

A simple count of where everything sits: awaiting approval, on hold, approved-but-unpaid, paid, completed, and rejected.

Value by project

Spend grouped by project, so you can see which events or campaigns are consuming the budget.

Recent requisitions

The latest items with their status — click any row to open it.

Use the menu (top bar, or the ☰ button on a phone) to move between Dashboard, Requisitions, Approvals, Projects, Vendors, Reports and — for admins — Admin.

04 The three request types

Choose the right type when you start. It determines the approval steps, the numbering, and how the item is paid.

TypeUse it when…ApprovalPaid to
FAM/REQPurchase requisitionYou're buying goods or services and need the full sign-off before committing.Full chainThe vendor
FAM/EXPReimbursable expenseA staff member paid out of their own pocket and needs to be paid back.Dept Head onlyThe staff member
FAM/CSRCredit service requestAn expense is incurred on credit — the vendor bills FAM later (e.g. a food or transport bill from an island).Dept Head firstThe vendor

The standard purchase approval chain runs top to bottom:

Department Head→ Finance Manager / Accounts (budget)→ General Secretary→ President / S.VP / VP

Each step must be signed before the next becomes active. Administrators can change the order, rename steps, add or remove them, or switch a step off from Admin → Approval chain — changes apply to new requisitions only.

Smaller purchases

If your administrator has turned on the low-value rule, purchases below a set amount need fewer quotations and only the approvers they selected. The requisition form always shows exactly which steps and how many quotations apply to your request.

05 Raising a purchase requisition

This is the core workflow. Only Finance and Admin roles create purchase requisitions.

  1. From the Dashboard or Requisitions page, choose New Requisition. The number shown while you type is provisional — the server confirms the final number (e.g. FAM/REQ/2026/012) the moment you save, so two people saving at once never get the same one.
  2. Set the date, department, and currency (for example MVR, USD, or CHF for FIFA payments). The chosen currency shows on every amount, print, and report.
  3. Pick a budget line from FAM's chart of accounts. If the spend is outside the budget, choose the Suspense line — it's flagged so finance can reallocate it later.
  4. Add a project (optional) for per-event cost tracking, and write a clear purpose.
  5. Add your items — description, quantity, and rate. The amount for each line is worked out for you.
  6. Choose the Tax: No tax, GST 8% or TGST 17% (tourism GST). Pick what the vendor charged — the total updates and every print shows the tax applied.
  7. Attach your quotations and mark the one you're going with (see the next section). The form shows how many quotations this request needs — usually three — and won't save until that many are attached with an amount.
  8. Set the payee (the vendor to be paid), prepared by, and the target days (your service-level target for the whole approval).
  9. Check the approval steps shown on the form, then choose Create requisition. It now enters the approval chain at the first step.
Editing

You can edit a requisition until approvals begin. After the first signature, the amounts and chain lock to preserve the audit trail — but you can still attach documents, record goods received, and record payment.

Repeat purchases

Buying the same thing again? Open a past requisition and choose the ⧉ (Duplicate) button at the top. You get a new requisition with today's date and the same items, payee and budget line, ready to review. Quotations and documents aren't copied — attach fresh ones.

06 Quotations & the AI reader

Attach the quotes you collected, and the system reads them, compares them against what matters for the request, and recommends the sensible choice.

Attaching & automatic reading

  1. In the Quotations area, choose Add quotation, then Attach the vendor's PDF or a photo of the quote.
  2. You'll see 🤖 Reading document…. Within a few seconds the row fills in the vendor, amount, delivery days, and a short note (payment terms / validity), read straight from the document.
  3. A message tells you exactly what was read — always check it against the document. It only fills fields you left blank and never overwrites something you typed.
Vendor matching

If the read vendor is already in your registry, it's linked automatically. If not, it shows as "Vendor name (unregistered)" — you can leave it, or register them properly with the ＋ button.

Choosing the best quote

Set "What matters for this request?" — the system recommends the best quote against it and marks it with a ★ Recommended badge:

  • Lowest price — the cheapest quote.
  • Fastest delivery — the quickest, using the delivery days on each quote.
  • Balanced — the best trade-off between price and delivery.

Mark your chosen quote as Selected. Two things happen automatically: the requisition amount is filled from the quote, and the payee is set to that vendor (only if you hadn't already entered them). If you pick a quote that isn't the recommended one, the system asks you to add a short justification that approvers will see.

Print the comparison

Open a saved requisition and choose Print quotation summary for a FAM-branded comparison sheet — every vendor, price vs. the lowest, delivery, the recommended pick, your justification, and sign-off boxes, with the quote images attached.

The AI reader is an optional feature that your IT team switches on. If it isn't reading your documents, simply enter the quote details by hand.

07 Reimbursable expenses

For when a staff member has already paid out of pocket and needs reimbursing — for example, a taxi or a small purchase made on the day.

  1. Choose Expense request. The number is FAM/EXP/….
  2. Fill in the purpose, the costs and the tax, and set the payee to the person being reimbursed.
  3. Submit it. Only the Department Head needs to approve.
  4. After approval, attach the receipts / invoices in the documents section.
  5. Finance records it as reimbursed once the money is paid back.
Who can raise one

Any signed-in staff member (except view-only accounts) can raise an expense request for themselves.

08 Credit service requests

For expenses incurred on credit, where a vendor will bill FAM later — such as a launch hire or a food bill for referees from an island, brought in weeks after the event.

The important difference from a reimbursement: the vendor is paid, not the staff, and approval must come first. The Department Head approves the staff to go ahead before the expense is incurred — so no one can bring in a bill later without a pre-approved request behind it.

Raise request→ HOD approves to proceed→ Awaiting invoice→ Bill received→ Convert to requisition & pay
  1. Choose Credit request (number FAM/CSR/…). Describe the expense, the estimated cost, the department, budget line, and the vendor if known.
  2. The Department Head approves. The request now reads Approved · awaiting invoice — so you can always see which credit expenses are still waiting for a bill.
  3. When the vendor's bill arrives, upload it in the documents section. The status becomes Invoice received.
  4. Choose Convert to purchase requisition. This creates a full FAM/REQ requisition, pre-filled from the credit request — items, vendor, budget line, tax and the bill — that runs the normal approval & payment flow. The original stays on record marked "Converted →".
Tracking them

The Credit services & invoices report lists every credit request and whether its bill has arrived — your running list of expenses you're still expecting invoices for.

09 Approving in the app

If you're an approver, the items waiting on your signature are gathered in one place.

  1. Open the Approvals tab. Your queue shows only the requisitions currently waiting on your step, with the number, purpose, project, and total.
  2. Choose Review to open the full requisition — items, quotations, budget line, and everything signed so far — or Approve directly from the queue.
  3. Inside a requisition, your step shows Approve & sign. Your signature is recorded electronically under your name and the date, marked ⚡ electronic.

The lower part of the Approvals tab lists all in-flight requisitions and who each is currently with, so anyone can see where things stand.

10 Approving by link (phone-friendly)

When an approver isn't a regular system user — or is simply out and about — Finance can send them a personal, one-click approval link.

  1. On the requisition, at the pending step, Finance chooses Send approval link and picks which registered person will sign.
  2. The link is personal to that person. Send it to them (copy it, or use the pre-written email). Don't forward it to anyone else.
  3. They open it on any phone or computer — no login. It shows the full requisition and reads "signing as [their name]".
  4. They choose Approve & sign, Reject, or Hold. The decision is recorded as "Electronically signed by [their name]" — on screen and on the printed form.
Built-in safeguard

The signer's identity is fixed when the link is created and cannot be changed from the approval page — so a signature always belongs to the right person. A link can be used once; after a final decision it stops working.

11 Signing on paper

Some approvers prefer to sign the printed form by hand. Finance records that signature in the system so the chain keeps moving and the audit file stays complete.

  1. Print the requisition and have the approver sign it.
  2. On the requisition, at the pending step, Finance chooses ✍️ Physical signature.
  3. Enter who signed and the date signed, and attach a scan or photo of the signed page (recommended — it's kept with the requisition's documents). Add a note if needed.
  4. Choose Record physical signature. The step is signed and marked ✍️ physical, and the next step becomes active.
Two kinds of signature

Every signed step shows how it was signed — ⚡ electronic (in the app or by link) or ✍️ physical (paper, recorded by Finance) — on screen and in the activity log.

12 Reject, hold & reopen

Approvers have more than a yes/no — you can pause an item or send it back with a reason.

Reject

Declines the requisition with a reason the requester sees. Use it when the request shouldn't proceed as-is.

Hold

Keeps the item pending on your step with a required reason (e.g. "waiting on a second quote"). You can still approve or reject it later.

Reopen

Clears a rejection or a hold and returns the step to pending so it can be decided again. The earlier comment stays on record.

Comments

Every decision can carry a note. Notes are kept with the requisition for the audit trail.

Reopening is available to the approver for that step, and to Finance and Admin — useful when a rejection was resolved and the item should move forward.

13 Goods received

Once a purchase is fully approved and delivered, record a Goods Received Note (GRN) — proof that FAM got what it paid for, before the vendor is paid.

  1. Open the fully approved purchase requisition and choose 📦 Record goods received. Finance, Admin, or the person who prepared the requisition can do this.
  2. Enter who received the goods, the date, and the vendor's delivery note reference.
  3. For each item, enter the quantity received and its condition — Good, Partial, Damaged or Missing. Add notes and attach the signed delivery note if you have it.
  4. Choose Save GRN. The requisition shows All received when everything arrived in good condition, or Partial / issues otherwise.
Record it before paying

Until a GRN is recorded, the requisition reminds Finance that the goods haven't been confirmed. Recording receipt first means FAM never pays for something that didn't arrive. Finance can edit a GRN later — for example when a back-order arrives.

14 Payment, Odoo & documents

Once a requisition is fully approved, Finance closes the loop.

  • Mark paid / reimbursed — record the payment with an optional reference (transfer or cheque number).
  • Post to Odoo — once paid, one click creates the vendor bill in Odoo (the vendor is found or created, and the items and tax are carried over). The Odoo reference is saved on the requisition. This needs Odoo to be connected by your administrator.
  • Mark manually — if you entered the bill in Odoo yourself, record its reference here instead.
  • Invoices & supporting documents — every requisition has a documents section. Upload the invoice, delivery note, or any paperwork so the complete audit file lives with the record.

A requisition reads Completed once it is fully approved, paid, and posted to Odoo.

Audit-ready

Because approvals, goods received, payment references, and documents all live on the one record, opening a requisition shows an auditor the whole story — who approved it, when, what arrived, what was paid, and against which invoice.

15 Combining expenses

When one person has several small reimbursements — say, multiple pickup hires in a week — Finance can pay them together as one requisition.

  1. On the Requisitions page choose 🧾 Combine expenses.
  2. Pick the staff member, then tick the unpaid expense requests you want to roll up. They must be in the same currency.
  3. Choose Combine selected. A single new expense requisition is created with all the line items and receipts together. Any GST or TGST on the originals is carried across as its own line, so the combined total matches exactly.

Each original is marked Combined into FAM/EXP/… so nothing is paid twice, and if every original was already approved, the combined one is ready for Finance to pay in one go.

16 Vendors

The vendor registry keeps supplier details in one place, so quotes and payees link cleanly.

  • Register a vendor with name, category, contact, and bank details for payment.
  • Upload the vendor's profile sheet and GST registration certificate — kept on file for compliance and viewable from any device.
  • The summary at the top shows how many vendors are registered and active, total spend to date, and how many have documents on file. Each vendor shows its own spend to date.
  • Find vendors fast with the search (name, contact, phone or email), the category buttons, and the active / inactive filter.

You can register a new vendor on the spot while filling a requisition — the ＋ button next to the payee or quote vendor — without losing your place in the form.

18 Activity log

Every requisition carries a complete, time-stamped history at the bottom of its page, under Activity.

  • When it was created and by whom, and any later edits.
  • Every signature — with who, when, and how it was given (⚡ via link, or ✍️ physical).
  • Goods received, payment recorded or changed, Odoo posting, and any renumbering.
Tamper-proof

The log is written by the server itself. No user of the system — not even an administrator — can edit or delete its entries, so it's a reliable record for auditors.

19 Admin settings Admin only

The Admin tab is where administrators configure the system. Changes apply to new requisitions — requisitions already raised keep the settings they were created with.

TabWhat you can set
NumberingThe prefix (e.g. FAM/REQ — expense and credit numbers follow automatically) and the next number for each type, so you can continue an existing paper sequence. Numbers never go below one already used, and restart at 001 each January.
TaxThe GST and TGST rates, and how many quotations a purchase requisition needs.
Approval chainThe signing order. For each step: the label on the form, the signer's title, and who may sign. Switch a step off to skip it, reorder with ↑ ↓, add new steps, or remove one entirely.
Low-value ruleOptional. Below an amount (sub-total, before tax), purchases need fewer quotations and only the approvers you tick.
Categories & listsVendor categories, currencies (★ sets the default) and departments. Add or remove entries — changes save immediately.
OdooThe Odoo address, database and username used by Post to Odoo, plus optional tax and journal IDs. Use Test connection to check it. The Odoo API key is installed on the server by your IT team and is never entered here.
UsersAdd, reset and remove accounts (below).

Managing users

  • Add a user — set their name, role, and which approval steps they may sign. The system generates a username and password, shown once — copy it and pass it on securely.
  • Reset a password — open the user, choose Reset password, and set a new one (or let the system generate one). It's shown once to share; the old one stops working immediately.
  • Delete a user — removes their access. Requisitions they signed keep their name on record.
Safety guards

You can't delete your own account, and you can't delete the last remaining administrator — so the organisation can never lock itself out.

21 Tips & FAQ

The number changed after I saved.

That's expected. The number shown while you type is provisional; the server confirms the final one when you save, so two people saving at once never collide.

The AI didn't read a field correctly.

It's an aid, not the final word. Just type over anything that's off — you're always asked to verify what it read.

A vendor quoted without GST.

Set Tax to No tax on the requisition. For resort or tourism suppliers charging TGST, choose TGST 17%.

Why can't I save my requisition?

Most often, it needs more quotations with an amount entered. The form tells you how many are required and how many you have.

An approver signed the printed form.

Finance records it with ✍️ Physical signature on that step, attaching a scan of the signed page.

Can an approver approve on their phone?

Yes — either by signing in, or via the personal approval link Finance sends. No app to install.

Someone brought a bill for an event with no paperwork.

That's what Credit service requests prevent — the Department Head must approve before the expense. Bills without a prior approved request shouldn't be entered.

How do I get a report for the auditors?

Reports tab → pick the report and period → Print / PDF → "Save as PDF", or Export CSV for a spreadsheet.

Need help?

For access, password resets, or a new account, contact your FAM system administrator.
